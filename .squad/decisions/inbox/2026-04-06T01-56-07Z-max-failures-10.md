## MAX_FAILURES: Raise Preview E2E from 5 → 10

**Requested by:** Parker  
**Approved by:** Ashley Hollis  
**Date:** 2026-04-06

### Decision
Raise `MAX_FAILURES` from 5 to 10 in preview E2E workflows (`.github/workflows/preview-e2e.yml`).

### Reason
LLM-based test generation introduces non-deterministic flakes in the setup phase (before LIVE_PROCESSING tests execute). A threshold of 5 is too aggressive and aborts runs that would pass if allowed to retry transient errors.

During this session, a single auth setup misconfiguration cascaded into 5 quick failures → premature abort → LIVE_PROCESSING tests never ran. With MAX_FAILURES=10, the workflow would have continued and surfaces the real issue instead of appearing to skip by design.

### Impact
- ✅ Allows transient flakes to resolve naturally
- ✅ Gives LIVE_PROCESSING tests opportunity to execute
- ✅ Better signal for real failures vs. flakes
- ⚠️ Slightly longer CI/CD time for some runs (acceptable given signal improvement)
